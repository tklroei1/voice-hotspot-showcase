The idea

The most important signal is the one humans miss

The micro-pause before a "yes". The energy dropping two questions ago. Enthusiastic words in a flat tone. Voice Hotspot catches those cues in real time and hands you a single, explainable next move — never a wall of transcript, only the moments that matter. The core bet: emotion read two independent ways is far more trustworthy than either alone.

What makes it interesting

Engineering highlights

🔀

Dual-signal fusion

A text layer (LLM + linguistic cues) and a voice layer (prosody) are computed independently, then combined with confidence weighting — with a dedicated stage that flags tone-vs-words contradiction.

📊

Calibrated, measured

Binary arousal is trained and evaluated speaker-independently on RAVDESS — honest numbers, not hand-waving. 83.7% shipped accuracy.

👤

Per-speaker baselining

A rolling baseline per speaker over the call: "is this turn more activated than their usual?" — the normalization that lifts accuracy.

🛡️

Resilient provider router

A fallback chain with debounce + circuit-breaker across Claude / Gemini / Groq / OpenRouter → local rules. Never hard-fails; a rate-limited free tier never spirals into 429s.

🧠

Domain-pack architecture

One analysis core + swappable taxonomy, framing, and local coaching rules per domain (Sales · Negotiation · Interview · General).

🧩

Chrome MV3 extension

Captures a Meet/Zoom tab's audio via tabCapture (offscreen document), runs the same prosody pipeline, scrapes live captions, and overlays a draggable prompter.
